Privacy

Effective October 3, 2026

The short version

This site and its simulations do not use cookies, analytics, trackers, ads or accounts, and they load nothing from other companies' servers. The matches run entirely in your browser. We do not sell or share your information, because we do not collect it.

What this page covers

Everything at starscream-agentics.github.io, including this splash page, Tank Arena, the Customizer and the field notes (the pages under /arena/).

Who runs this site

Starscream Agentics is the simulations lab of Coastal Agentics, the planned name of a company in Savannah, Georgia, that has not been formed yet. Until it is, this site is run by Nye Warburton ("we", "us").

What stays in your browser

Tank Arena and the Customizer run on our own engine, downloaded from this site as WebAssembly and run on your device. Your choices, such as the seed, each Nyborg's behavior and its build, are kept in the page address (the URL) so that you can share a link to a match. Nothing is saved in your browser's storage, and nothing you pick is sent to us.

If you share a link, anyone who opens it can see the setup it contains. Links hold only match settings, not personal information.

What the site downloads

Pages fetch their own files from this same site, such as the engine file and the field notes list. These requests go to GitHub Pages like any other page load; they carry no information about you beyond what any web request does.

Hosting: GitHub Pages

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit any GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs your IP address and stores it for security purposes. We do not receive those logs and cannot see who visits or plays. GitHub's handling of that data is covered by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

Pages here link to GitHub and to coastal-agentics.github.io. Those sites have their own privacy terms.

Contacting us

For now, the way to reach us is a GitHub issue, which is public and covered by GitHub's own terms and privacy statement. If you write to us, we receive whatever you choose to send. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation, and we do not add you to a mailing list or share your message with anyone else, except the services that carry our messages, or when the law requires it. Ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13. It has no accounts, chat or sign-up, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If what this site collects changes, for example if we add accounts, online play, cloud saves or analytics, we will update this page and its effective date before the change goes live.

Governing law

This page is governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, USA.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please don't put personal information in them. We will publish a private email address here once our own domain is set up.
