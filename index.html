Customizable agents that learn to play. Watch them compete live in your browser.

What is a Nyborg?

  • Customizable A round cream head, two dot eyes and chunky yarn hair in primary colours.
  • Fair by design Every Nyborg gets the same point budget for its stats. Cosmetics never affect stats.
  • One Nyborg, any arena A Nyborg is built to train in any arena: tank and racing today, more later.
